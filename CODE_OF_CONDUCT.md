# Code of conduct

All participants in issues, pull requests, reviews and other Helseark project
spaces must treat one another with respect, regardless of identity, background,
experience or disability. This applies to maintainers and contributors alike.

Be constructive and specific. Critique work without attacking people, accept
corrections, respect boundaries, and help people participate. Harassment, threats,
hate speech, sexual attention, doxxing, intimidation and repeated disruptive
behavior are not acceptable. Never share another person's health information or
private communications without permission.

Report concerns privately to the [repository owner](https://github.com/ricardocabral)
through an available private contact. If a private channel is not available,
request one with a minimal issue containing no names, allegations or sensitive
details. Do not post incident evidence publicly. A tested private conduct contact
and an alternate for concerns involving the owner must be established before
public launch; see the [release checklist](docs/maintainers/releasing.md).

Maintainers will review available evidence fairly, limit disclosure to what is
needed, and may ask for corrections, remove content, restrict participation or
ban participants according to severity and repetition. Maintainers should recuse
themselves from decisions involving their own conduct. Appeals may be requested
through the same private route and reviewed by an uninvolved maintainer when one
is available. This policy does not promise confidentiality beyond what the
reporting platform and necessary investigation allow.

This is a project-specific policy, not an adoption of an external conduct template.
